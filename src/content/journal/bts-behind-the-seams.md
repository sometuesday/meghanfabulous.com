It all began when my mom taught me how to sew at the age of four. From that moment on, design became my playground, and my mission has always been to create wearable works of art that make everyone, no matter their shape or size, feel fabulous.

Then came my inspiration for the officially licensed Grateful Dead Fabulous Collection.

In high school, I began collection Grateful Dead t-shirts, deconstructed them, reconstructed them, beaded them, and transformed them into dresses that carried the spirit of the music I adored.

In 2004, Paris Hilton wore my Grateful Dead dress to the VH1 Big in '04 Awards (December 2004, Shrine Auditorium, Los Angeles).

And just like that, my career was launched, leading to the release of my officially licensed collection in 2023. A dream fueled by decades of love and inspiration from the spirit of the music.
