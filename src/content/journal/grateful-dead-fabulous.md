## The dress, December 2004

In the spring of 2003, in her mid-twenties, Meghan had lost the right to use her own name in business. She spent the summer at the beach, beading a thrifted Grateful Dead T-shirt into a dress: lace, hand-dyed silk, butterflies, a hand-sewn zipper. A stylist borrowed it. Paris Hilton wore it to the VH1 Big in '04 Awards (December 2004, Shrine Auditorium, Los Angeles).

The photograph is not on this site. [See the photo](https://liveforlivemusic.com/features/touch-of-glam-meghan-fabulous-grateful-dead/) in James Sissler’s LiveForLiveMusic feature (25 July 2024). Photograph by Jeff Kravitz.

That night restarted the career. The licensed line came much later. She has said the licence conversation with Warner began in May 2021, and Relix reported the collection in 2025 as officially licensed, launched in 2023, with a second collection in June 2024.

## Where the art started

She found *Steal Your Face* at Music Market, a record shop in Orange County, in middle school. The music took longer. In 1993, her sophomore year, she went to Haight-Ashbury. The full telling is in her own essay from April 2023.

Steve came the other way. In a note from May 2023 he wrote that the music found him first, around Franklin’s Tower and a show at Hampton Coliseum, and that when he asked Meghan if she knew the Grateful Dead she answered with the art, including that dress. They met in Santa Monica in the summer of 2018. Her first Dead & Company show was Dodger Stadium, July 2018.

Alex Khatchadourian told the same meeting in the *Easy Reader* on 3 October 2023: [Love and luster on Shakedown Street](https://easyreadernews.com/meghan-fabulous-el-grateful-dead-el-segundo-art-walk/).

## Summer 2023, the sky, and the road

At the Dead & Company shows in the summer of 2023, a drone show put glittering Stealies and dancing bears in the sky. She has written two versions of what she did next. One became the sequin Dancing Bears set. The other, told in [The Birth of My Best Selling Stealie](/journal/the-birth-of-my-best-selling-stealie), was the rhinestone Stealie.

![Dancing bears, drawn as if seen from above.](/images/journal/grateful-dead-fabulous/Bears_at_sea_1.webp)

![The sequin dancing-bear set.](/images/journal/grateful-dead-fabulous/Dancing_Bear_boat.webp)

That same summer she took the clothes to the shows. Los Angeles opened the run, about ten minutes from home, and she wrote that seeing the pieces on other people was the feeling she worked for. Philadelphia followed, with LP Giobbi’s after-party and a Shakedown full of friends. San Francisco, she said, was where the week became about the people more than the launch.
