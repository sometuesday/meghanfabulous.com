For all my Astrology Queens, I've customized a sunnies horoscope just for you!

1.  Aries:  Firecracker Funglasses:   Fierce, ambitious and passionate, you thrive under pressure and love a challenge.  To be the boss and live life to the extreme, oh and did I mention to be the boss?

![Photograph from “Sunnies Written In The Stars”](/images/journal/2025-05-15-sunnies-written-in-the-stars/Aries.webp)

2.  Taurus:  Champagne Popper Funglasses:  Sensual, affectionate, and loyal to a fault, you are the most reliable but sometimes your stubborn streak holds you back.  Find someone that will force you out of your shell and live beyond your wildest.

3.  Gemini:  Black White Read all-ova Funglasses:   Passionate and flirty, you love to dish out compliments and loads of charm.  Find someone that loves all of your different personalities, the good, bad and the crazy.

![Photograph from “Sunnies Written In The Stars”](/images/journal/2025-05-15-sunnies-written-in-the-stars/Gemini.webp)

4.  Cancer:  Bubbles Rhinestone Funglasses - white neon:   Emotional, guarded, and imaginative.  You need to find a way to stop being so neurotic and find your inner zen.

![Photograph from “Sunnies Written In The Stars”](/images/journal/2025-05-15-sunnies-written-in-the-stars/Cancer.webp)

5.  Leo:  Gem Bomb Funglasses:  Popular, charismatic, and more perceptive than you let on.  Your high expectations, and attention-seeking demeanor demands the spotlight.  Shine baby, shine on!!

![Photograph from “Sunnies Written In The Stars”](/images/journal/2025-05-15-sunnies-written-in-the-stars/Leo.webp)

6.  Virgo: Crystal Bomb Funglasses:   You are a stickler for detail, and you can't let things go.  Sarcasm is your defense mechanism, and if everyone would just do it your way then the world would just be a better place.

7.  Libra: Dream Girl Funglasses:  The Queen Bee, the ultimate Femme Fatale.  You make others feel special.  If only you could just lie around in piles of rose petals all day, things would be dandy.

![Photograph from “Sunnies Written In The Stars”](/images/journal/2025-05-15-sunnies-written-in-the-stars/Libra.webp)

8.  Scorpio: Peaches & Urb Funglasses:   Ultra passionate, thoughtful and seeks admiration.  To overcome obstacles and find true happiness, you should just have more fun, sexy encounters!

9.  Sagittarius:  Bunny Babe Funglasses:   Adventurous and magnetic.  You are the charm connoisseur, and you know you are perfect, it's pretty obvious now, isn't it?

10.  Capricorn:  Groovy Baby Funglasses - Pink Neon:   You are the obvious operator, a true independent.  You analyze everything, everyone, everyplace and then go over it again just to be sure.  I mean, someone has to do it the right way.

![Photograph from “Sunnies Written In The Stars”](/images/journal/2025-05-15-sunnies-written-in-the-stars/Capricorn.webp)

11.  Aquarius: The Earth Goddess Funglasses:   Find someone that thinks your weird is beautiful.  You are goofy, unconventional, and reserved.  You are not meant for everyone, but those someones will for sure be your everything.

![Photograph from “Sunnies Written In The Stars”](/images/journal/2025-05-15-sunnies-written-in-the-stars/Aquarius.webp)

12.  Pisces: Groovy Baby Funglasses - Lime Green:   You feel EVERYTHING and your creative nature longs to live in a world of dreams that never become reality.  Never stop imagining as this is what makes you so appealing.

![Photograph from “Sunnies Written In The Stars”](/images/journal/2025-05-15-sunnies-written-in-the-stars/Pisces.webp)
