*Originally posted 25 September 2025, when Meghan closed the Meghan Fabulous business. Updated once the voyage was under way.*

I have some incredibly exciting (and personal) news to share with you all.

After years of building Meghan Fabulous, I'm about to set sail on the adventure of a lifetime. Steve and I are taking our boat, *Some Tuesday*, and sailing around the world! ⛵️🌊

For years, Meghan Fabulous has been my heart and soul: created for you, inspired by you, and built with your feedback and love. It has been such an honor to design pieces that make you feel fabulous and confident. You have been my biggest muses.

I would love for you to continue this journey with me, and together we'll explore more than just fashion. We'll explore the world.

Thank you from the bottom of my heart for being part of this journey. Your love and support mean the world to me, and I can't wait to share what's next!

With all my love,
Meghan

---

**Update, 2026.** On December 3, 2025, Steve and I departed Los Angeles aboard *Some Tuesday*, our Lagoon 450S, with our pugs Colonel Mustard and Mr. Pickles, the first pugs to cross the Pacific on a private sailing yacht. We sailed south through Mexico to El Salvador, crossed the Pacific in April 2026 (28 days at sea) to the Marquesas, and we're exploring the South Pacific now. I'm making again, just for joy.
