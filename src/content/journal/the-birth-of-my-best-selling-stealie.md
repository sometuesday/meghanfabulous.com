I really had no idea what the follow up act to the iconic Original Grateful Dead Fabulous Collection was going to be.

It needed to be equally as bold, but with a more broad appeal...something that no other designer had done or created with the Grateful Dead Brand.

It needed to stay true to the band and music, while speaking to the fabulous women that loved it so much.

![Photograph from “The Birth of My Best Selling Stealie”](/images/journal/2025-05-30-the-birth-of-my-best-selling-stealie/IMG_0063.webp)

There Steve and I were, sitting in the stadium at the Summer 2023 shows when the drone show popped into the sky. Glittering Stealies with sparkling bears dancing through the sky ignited a spar that hit me like a ton of bricks.

It was so clear, and I couldn't race home fast enough to develop what has now become the "Best Selling Rhinestone Stealie of All Time."
