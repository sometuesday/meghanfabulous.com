On 3 December 2025 they cast off from Los Angeles. The boat is *Some Tuesday*, a Lagoon 450S. Colonel Mustard and Mr. Pickles came too: the first pugs to cross the Pacific on a private sailing yacht.

The route so far is Mexico, El Salvador, the Marquesas after twenty-eight days at sea, the South Pacific, and, as of September 2026, Tonga. Ahead, as they have drawn it: Australia and New Zealand, the Indian Ocean, the Mediterranean, the Caribbean in 2029, and home by the Panama Canal in 2030.

She is still making. The bags are on [Making Now](/making-now). None of them are for sale.

Their former home was Manhattan Beach, California. The departure was Los Angeles.
