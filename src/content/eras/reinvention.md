The middle years were a second start. The jumpsuit. The line she named BOHEME. The trademark, back in her hands. And, in 2018, Steve.

The portrait on this page was made later, in 2021. These years do not have their own campaign in the archive. The work does: the Wild Orchid jumpsuit is on the press page, and the Dark Star kimono is in the journal.
