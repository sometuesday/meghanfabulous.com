The clothes left Los Angeles. Drapers counted the British doors. The Jamaica Observer printed the wider list, in her own words. Tokyo kept putting the prints on the page.

A partnership ended in these years and she became a one-woman show. That is the whole of that sentence.

The lookbooks here are undated, the same rule as on the runway page. They are shown for the clothes, not for a season.
