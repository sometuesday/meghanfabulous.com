There is no runway photograph from these years in the archive. The record is on paper.

She had been sewing since she was four. Her mother taught her by hand. The machine came at six. The first company was hers in 2001. A few years later the company, and the right to sell under her own name, were not.

She does not tell that chapter as a grievance. She tells it as the moment the next name arrived. A friend told her to change it. She wanted something fabulous. Meghan Fabulous is the name that stuck.

The dress that carried the new name into public is a later story, and a Grateful Dead one. It belongs to the [last era but one](/eras/grateful-dead-fabulous), and to [the journal](/journal/the-paris-hilton-grateful-dead-dress).
