Los Angeles Fashion Week was the public start of the new name. The clothes were already what they would stay: print, bead, a joke, a little too much.

The lookbooks on this page are not dated and not credited. They were made across the years after these runways, and they live here because this is where the silhouette started. The rest of them are on [The world](/eras/the-world). All of them, by mood, are in the [lookbooks](/lookbooks).
